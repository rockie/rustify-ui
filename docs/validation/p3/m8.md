## The negative probes, which are the point

A frame budget measured on animation-frame callbacks passes a frozen screen,
because callbacks keep arriving at 60 Hz in front of one. So before either
frame gate takes a figure it runs five seconds in which the drive continues and
the content does not change, and that run **has to miss the budget**:

| Probe | Drives | Callbacks | Presentations | Samples | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| B2, content frozen | 301 | 302 | **0** | **0** | does not hold |
| B3, scene frozen | 601 | 602 | **0** | **0** | does not hold, with **601 pumps** |

B3's pump count is the one worth reading twice. The region was pumping and
receiving props for all 601 drives and declined only to apply the camera, so
the picture stopped while everything around it kept running — which is exactly
the failure a callback-based gate would sail through.

The two probes freeze their content differently, and only one needed the
application's help. B3 uses `freeze_scene(true)`, because the camera comes from
the application. B2 needs no switch: the table's content version is a function
of where it is scrolled to, so re-driving the same scroll position leaves the
picture genuinely unchanged. §9.3 provided for a `freeze_table(true)` and it
was not built, because the honest version of it already exists in the driver.

## What the checks found

**A label list with a catch-all arm reports the wrong name.** The verify
suite's step names were chosen by matching the example against a fixed list
with `_ =>` at the end. Adding a fourth example made the report say
`double build: property-workbench` twice and never mention `data-workbench`;
the browser step was worse, and would have reported `data-workbench`,
`budget-minimal` and `budget-data` all as `browser: deployment`. The builds and
the runs were correct — only the report was lying, which is the worst place for
it. `Step.name` is now built from the name it is about.

**`recomputes == drives` held exactly, which was not obvious.** Scroll events
are dispatched asynchronously, so the last driven frame's recompute could
plausibly have landed after the final reading and made the assertion off by
one. Across five 60-second runs it was 3,601 = 3,601 every time, so the
assertion stays exact rather than being loosened to a tolerance that would hide
a real drop.

**B3 is measured against the display, not the budget.** Sixty seconds produced
7,201 effective presentations — 120 per second, because this machine's display
runs at 120 Hz. The physical floor for an interval is 8.33 ms and the
measurement is 9.30. A reader comparing 9.30 ms against a 60 Hz machine's
16.7 ms floor would draw the wrong conclusion, so the report says which.

**Three projects, not two.** §10's M8 row asked for two. §7 asks for B2's gate
to run headless and B3's to run on headed Chrome only, and one Playwright
project has one `headless` setting. The environment rule is the one with a
measurement behind it (probe 2: 50.10 ms under SwiftShader against 17.60 ms
headed), so the split follows the environment: `budget-minimal` and
`budget-data` headless and in CI, `budget-scene` headed, by hand, in neither CI
nor the verify suite. §10 and §9.5 are amended.

**The component needed one test seam.** The table's scroller is the one element
in it a caller cannot reach by role or label, and the frame gate has to drive
it. It now carries `{name}-scroller`, in the same pattern as the
`{name}-row-{slot}` and `{name}-column-{index}` ids it already emitted.

## A-4: not performed

The form is at `docs/validation/p3/manual/voiceover-data.md` and says
`STATUS: NOT PERFORMED`. It asks for VoiceOver + Chrome over B2's three rows
(1, 50,000 and 100,000) and B3's three objects, with what was said recorded per
step.

What the automated checks establish is that the tree is correct: row indices
are the rows' own out of a hundred thousand, one tab stop per grid, the query
entries reach every target, and each target can be opened and changed from the
keyboard. What they cannot establish is whether the speech makes a hundred
thousand rows **navigable** rather than merely announced.

`cargo xtask verify --suite p3` prints it as a record not handed in and ends
with "P3 cannot be called complete while a manual record is missing." The user
may waive it as they waived P2's remaining VoiceOver rows; a waiver is recorded
as **untested, waived**, never as a pass.
